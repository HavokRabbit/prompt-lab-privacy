Prompt Lab Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026
Developer: Iron Vespers / HavokRabbit

Prompt Lab is an offline educational app that teaches kids (about ages 8–12) how to write clear prompts for AI tools. This policy explains what the app does — and does not — do with information.

Short version

  • Prompt Lab does not collect personal information from children or adults.
  • The app works offline. Lessons and videos stay on the device.
  • There are no ads, no trackers, and no third-party analytics SDKs.
  • There is no account sign-in and no cloud sync of progress.

Information we collect

We do not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information. Prompt Lab does not ask for a child’s name, email, photo, location, contacts, or other identifiers.

Data stored on the device

Progress (for example, which lessons were finished and any certificate status) is stored only in the app’s local storage on that phone or tablet. Uninstalling the app removes that local data. We cannot see or recover it.

Internet use

Prompt Lab is designed to run without an internet connection after install. Lesson media is packaged with the app (including Play Asset Delivery where used). The app does not upload lesson answers, progress, or device identifiers to our servers.

Ads, analytics, and third parties

Prompt Lab does not show ads and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers.

Children’s privacy (COPPA / Families)

Prompt Lab is intended for families and children in the approximate age range of 8–12, with grown-up / parent options for settings. Because we do not collect personal information, we do not knowingly obtain personal data from children. If you believe a child has somehow provided personal information to us outside the app (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

Permissions

The app only requests permissions needed to play local lesson media and store progress on the device. It does not require access to the camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location for core features.
